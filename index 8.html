STEP 1 · READ

Meet Jisoo

Read the short story. Then click the expressions to see their meanings.

Jisoo wanted to improve her study habits before the exam period. She began to keep track of how long she studied each day. She also learned when to take a break instead of studying for too long. While studying, she tried to stay away from her phone and made sure to get enough sleep. When she felt stressed, she learned how to deal with it by taking a short walk. After several weeks, she found it easier to stick to her new routine.
keep track of

→ 계속 확인하다 / 기록하다
take a break

→ 잠시 쉬다
stay away from

→ ~을 피하다 / 멀리하다
get enough sleep

→ 충분히 자다
deal with

→ ~을 처리하다 / 대처하다
stick to

→ ~을 계속 지키다

Six expressions to remember

keep track of — 계속 확인하다 / 기록하다
take a break — 잠시 쉬다
stay away from — ~을 피하다 / 멀리하다

💡 Study Tip: Good study habits are not about studying all the time. Take breaks, get enough sleep, stay away from distractions, and stick to a routine!
